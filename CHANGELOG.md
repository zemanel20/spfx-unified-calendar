# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-17)

First public package.

- Outlook events, Planner tasks and To Do tasks in one grid, with a chip per source.
- Month, week and agenda views; week, three days and month in narrow columns and on phones.
- Working-week settings: days of the week shown, hours of the week view, 24-hour clock.
- Planner scope: tasks assigned to me, or every task in my plans.
- Completed tasks visible by default, dimmed and struck through, with a toggle to hide them.
- Click preview with the deep link to Outlook, Planner or To Do.
- Theme, dark mode, Brand Center fonts and page language followed automatically.
- Delegated Microsoft Graph permissions only: `Calendars.Read`, `Tasks.Read`.
